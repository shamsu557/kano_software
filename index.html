Assignment Late Submission Policy

Assignments must be submitted by the due date. Late submissions attract mark deductions as shown below:

Day 1
−20% of total marks deducted for submitting 1 day late.
Day 2
−30% total — each additional day after day 1 attracts −10%.
Day 3+
−10% per day continues to accumulate until submission or zero.

Maximum deduction is 100% of the assignment marks. Submitting is always better than not submitting.

Policies

Terms & Conditions

Financial Policies
  • Registration fees are non-refundable.
  • Fees may be paid in installments — confirm with admin.
  • Graduation fee is charged separately at programme end.
  • Your certificate is released only after full payment is confirmed.
Laptop & Attendance
  • Students must bring their own laptop every session.
  • Attendance is compulsory — minimum attendance required.
  • All assignments must be attempted and submitted.
  • Minimum 50% overall score is required to graduate.
Submission Policy
  • Submit assignments before the due date to avoid deductions.
  • 1 day late → 20% of marks deducted.
  • Each day after the first → additional 10% deducted.
  • Always submit — partial marks are better than zero.
Conduct & Ethics
  • All submitted work must be your own original code.
  • Plagiarism results in disqualification from that assignment.
  • Respect tutors, fellow students, and the learning environment.
  • AI tools may be used but you must understand every line you submit.
